What every circuit includes

Walked, not packaged.

Six commitments shape every itinerary we publish. They are why monasteries open their gates to us, and why pilgrims return.

Scholar-guides

A guide trained at Nalanda, Drepung, or Komazawa — fluent in sutra, history, and the local tongue.

Lineage-verified routes

Every itinerary reviewed by resident monks at Mahabodhi, Hemis, and Eihei-ji before a seat is offered.

Twelve seats, no more

The places we visit cannot hold a crowd; the teachings we receive will not arrive in one.

All-inclusive pricing

Boutique heritage stays, all meals, all transport, monastery donations, teaching fees, visas. No surprises.

Carbon-conscious travel

Overland between sacred sites where possible; offsets through verified Bihar reforestation. Disclosed.

Pre-trip dharma reading

Curated reading list, weekly online sessions with your guide for six weeks before departure.
