Sacred Sites

The four great places — and the journeys that connect them.

Each destination is a chapter in the Buddha's life. We walk them slowly, with local teachers, real history, and time to sit.

Bodhgaya

The seat of awakening. Mahabodhi Temple, the Bodhi tree, and the inner stillness that draws pilgrims from every tradition.

Sarnath

Where the first teaching was given. Dhamek Stupa, the deer park, and the turning of the wheel of Dharma.

Lumbini

The birthplace, in the Nepalese Terai. Mayadevi Temple, the Ashoka pillar, and the international monastic zone.

Kushinagar

The place of mahaparinirvana. The reclining Buddha, the cremation stupa, and the quiet that follows.

Rajgir & Nalanda

The great university and the Vulture Peak. Where philosophy, debate, and meditation met for a thousand years.

Varanasi

The eternal city. Ghats at dawn, the Ganga aarti at dusk, and the gateway to Sarnath just beyond.
