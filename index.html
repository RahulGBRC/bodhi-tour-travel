Why Bodhi Dragon

Walked, not packaged.

Four commitments shape every itinerary we publish. They are why monasteries open their gates to us, and why pilgrims return.

Scholar-guides

Every circuit is led by a guide trained at Nalanda, Drepung, or Komazawa — fluent in sutra, history, and the local tongue.

Lineage-verified routes

Every itinerary is reviewed by resident monks at Mahabodhi, Hemis, and Eihei-ji before a single seat is offered.

Small groups, always

Twelve pilgrims, no more. The places we visit cannot hold a crowd; the teachings we receive will not arrive in one.

Carbon-conscious travel

Overland between sacred sites where possible; offsets purchased through verified Bihar reforestation. Disclosed in every itinerary.
