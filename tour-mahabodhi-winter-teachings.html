The journey

Once a year, when the great teachings are given at Bodhgaya, the town becomes the centre of the Buddhist world. Pilgrims arrive from every tradition and every country; the Mahabodhi grounds fill before dawn.

Attending well, in that density, takes preparation and relationships built over decades. We hold a reserved monastery close to the temple, secure morning audience seats, and travel with a senior translator so the teachings arrive in English as they are given. Between sessions the group keeps a quiet practice schedule, and our guide walks the seven sacred spots with anyone who wishes.

The same tree, and beneath it, the whole world listening.

This journey runs to the calendar of the teachings and is offered by invitation, with priority to pilgrims who have walked with us before. Dates confirm once the teaching schedule is announced.

Day by day

  1. Day 1

    Arrive Bodhgaya

    Met at Gaya and settled into our reserved monastery near the Mahabodhi. An orientation to the days ahead and the forms of the teaching ground.

    Bodhgaya
  2. Day 2

    First morning teaching

    An early walk to the audience area for the opening session, returning to the monastery for lunch, rest and a translation review with our teacher.

    Bodhgaya
  3. Day 3

    Teaching · and the bodhi tree

    Morning teaching, then an afternoon at the bodhi tree and the Vajrasana while the grounds are quieter, sitting where the awakening is said to have occurred.

    Bodhgaya
  4. Day 4

    Teaching · the seven weeks walk

    Morning teaching, and an afternoon walking the seven sacred spots of the seven weeks after the awakening, in order, with commentary.

    Bodhgaya
  5. Day 5

    Teaching · monastery row

    Morning teaching, then the international monasteries — a chance to see how each tradition holds the same event, closing at the Great Buddha statue.

    Bodhgaya
  6. Day 6

    Closing teaching · dedication

    The final session and the dedication of merit, then a quiet evening of reflection and a closing circumambulation of the temple by butter-lamp.

    Bodhgaya
  7. Day 7

    Depart Bodhgaya

    A closing sit and breakfast, then transfers to Gaya for onward flights, or connection to our India circuit for those continuing.

Before you book

Questions pilgrims ask.

Why is this journey by invitation?

The teaching days draw enormous crowds, and attending well depends on reserved space and relationships we protect carefully. We give priority to returning pilgrims and to those continuing onto the India circuit, so the group stays small and the monastery can receive us.

Are the exact dates confirmed?

They confirm once the teaching schedule is publicly announced, usually a few months ahead. The window is reliably late December into early January; we hold the reservation and notify enquirers as soon as dates are set.

Do I need prior teaching experience?

No, but this is a contemplative week rather than an introduction. The pre-trip sessions and our translator's daily reviews help newer pilgrims follow the material; a willingness to sit with difficulty helps most of all.

Can I combine it with the India circuit?

Yes — many pilgrims attach the winter teachings to the start or end of the fourteen-day circuit. Tell us and we will arrange the connection as a single journey.
