What we do

Twelve services, one promise.

Beyond the itinerary, every Bodhi pilgrimage includes the small, careful things that turn a trip into a real journey.

❖

Pilgrimage Planning

Detailed itineraries built around your group's tradition, dates, and energy level. Pre-departure briefings, packing guides, and a dedicated coordinator.

⌂

Accommodation

Clean, well-located hotels close to the temples. Rooms are inspected each season. Special arrangements for monks, elders, and dietary needs.

→

Transport

Air-conditioned vehicles with experienced drivers. Domestic flights coordinated. Train tickets in higher classes. Border crossings to Nepal handled end-to-end.

☼

Local Guides

Site-specific guides at every major place — scholars, retired professors, and practitioners. Not generalists, but people who know each stupa and sutra.

☱

Dharma Access

Coordination for teachings, audiences with senior teachers, monastery visits, and access during festival seasons. Winter teachings passes in Bodhgaya.

✿

Vegetarian Catering

All meals vegetarian by default; vegan and Jain options available. Filtered drinking water always provided. We know the safe restaurants in every town.

✚

Medical Support

First-aid kit on every vehicle. Doctor on call in Bodhgaya, Varanasi, and Lumbini. Travel insurance partnerships for international pilgrims.

⚐

Visa & Permits

India e-visa guidance, Nepal entry support, and permits for restricted areas (Sikkim, Spiti, Tabo). We don't apply for you, but we walk you through it.

☷

Group Logistics

Sangha tours from Thailand, Vietnam, Korea, Sri Lanka, and beyond. Translators where needed. Coordination with home temples for offerings and ceremonies.

◇

Photography & Records

Group photographer available on request. Daily journal templates. Many pilgrims return with a record they keep for life.

♡

Care for Elders

Wheelchair-accessible vehicles. Slower itineraries. Walking sticks and shaded rest at every stupa. Many of our pilgrims are 70+ and we're proud of that.

★

Custom Ceremonies

Lighting offerings at the Mahabodhi, robe offerings at Kushinagar, alms offerings at Sarnath. Coordinated with local sangha and temple authorities.
