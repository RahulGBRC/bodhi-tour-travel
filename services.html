How a journey is made

Four steps, six weeks.

Whether you join a published circuit or commission a private one, the path from first enquiry to first morning at the temple looks the same.

Conversation

A 30-minute call with the planner who will hold your journey end-to-end. We ask about practice, group, season, and what is calling.

Draft itinerary

Within two working days, a draft itinerary — places, pace, monasteries, costs. Iterated with you until the route feels right.

Pre-trip practice

Six weeks of weekly online sessions with your scholar-guide. Reading list, sutra study, and travel logistics together in one call.

The walk

You arrive in Bodhgaya. We take care of everything — transport, monasteries, meals, donations — so you can simply walk and sit.

Frequently asked

Practical matters.

The questions every pilgrim asks. If yours isn't here, write to us and we will answer within two working days.

Do I need to be Buddhist to join a circuit?

No. Roughly half of our pilgrims are not Buddhist by formal commitment. The places we visit and the teachers we walk with welcome anyone who comes with respect and a willingness to listen. The pre-trip reading we provide makes the experience much richer regardless of your starting point.

How physically demanding are the circuits?

The India and Sri Lanka circuits are easy — short walks of 1–3 km a day, mostly flat. The Ladakh circuit involves altitudes of 3500–5300m and requires reasonable fitness. The Japan circuit includes seated meditation periods of 30–45 minutes. We are happy to discuss your specific situation before booking.

What is included in the price?

All accommodation (twin share, boutique heritage stays), all meals, all ground transport, scholar-guide throughout, monastery donations, teaching fees, entrance fees, visa support and travel insurance briefing. Not included: international flights, personal travel insurance, alcohol, optional extra activities.

Can I extend the trip with personal travel?

Yes. Many pilgrims extend with a few days in Delhi, the Taj Mahal in Agra, or Kerala backwaters in the south. We are happy to arrange these extensions through our trusted partners — billed separately from the circuit.

What if I need to cancel?

Full refund up to 90 days before departure. 50% refund up to 60 days. We strongly recommend comprehensive travel insurance covering trip cancellation. We are happy to make a single date-change at no charge if circumstances require.
